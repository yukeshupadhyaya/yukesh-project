01 / About

Engineer's mind, builder's hands

Originally from Baglung, Nepal, I came to the United States to chase a simple goal: to design and build things that make life better for people. Today I'm a sophomore in Civil Engineering at Minnesota State University, Mankato, working through the core of statics, mechanics of materials, and structural design.

Before civil engineering, I studied Information Technology — and I never left the code behind. I write in Python, C#, and Java, I've designed digital hardware in SystemVerilog, and I'm comfortable everywhere from CAD to the command line. That dual background is my edge: I don't just run the analysis, I can automate it.

I've spent two years as a Student Manager at Sodexo, leading a team in one of the busiest spots on campus, and I mentor young children as a Teaching Aide. I'm detail-obsessed, calm under pressure, and always shipping — and right now I'm looking for a civil engineering internship where I can put all of it to work.
