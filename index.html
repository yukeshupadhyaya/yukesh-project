Currently researching

MavTap

Your phone is your campus key.

Planned foriPhone · App StoreAndroid · Google Play

MavTap is my idea for an app on both iPhone and Android that lets Minnesota State students tap their phone on an NFC reader to get access on campus, the same way you'd tap to pay. Only real Mavericks get in, because every account is verified with an MNSU email. It's my own idea and my first mobile app, and I'm researching it properly before I write the first line of code.

1The problem

One more thing to carry

Students juggle a physical card for campus access, and a card is easy to forget or lose. A phone almost never gets left behind.

2The idea

Tap with your phone

Use the NFC chip already inside most iPhones and Android phones so a quick tap does the same job as the card.

3The trust layer

Mavericks only

Sign up with an MNSU email, so every account belongs to a real student before it can open anything.

What I'm figuring out

  • How iPhone and Android NFC can each act like an access card, and the rules Apple and Google set for it
  • The cleanest way to confirm a student owns their MNSU email
  • What campus readers and systems would need to talk to the app
  • How to keep student data private and stop anyone from copying a pass
